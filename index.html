How I work

Curious enough to investigate. Collaborative enough to ask.

Being new to an industry taught me to be resourceful without pretending to have every answer. When I spotted a customer issue or process gap, I gathered background information, researched possible solutions, and organized what I learned into a clear starting point.

I brought findings and preliminary recommendations to leadership and more experienced colleagues, invited their input, and refined the next steps together. That combination of independent initiative, thoughtful questions, and openness to feedback helped me adapt quickly and contribute while building my expertise.
