About me

A different path.
A useful perspective.

I believe the strongest customer relationships start when people feel heard, supported, and genuinely cared for.

My move from healthcare into tech sales was intentional. I spent nearly two years working toward the transition, but one thing never changed: the part of my work I value most is building relationships with people.

In healthcare, I regularly saw the same people and got to know them over time. That experience taught me how meaningful it is to become someone others feel comfortable turning to. At Chmura, I found that same sense of purpose in Customer Success. I loved getting to know customers, understanding their challenges, and becoming someone they could trust—not just another person trying to sell them something.

I bring an adaptable, positive mindset and a belief that good service is about more than having an immediate answer. It's about following through, communicating openly, and making sure people know you're in their corner.

How I work

The things I want customers
to count on.

These are the qualities I try to bring to every relationship—with customers and teammates alike.

01 / ADVOCACY

Understand first.
Advocate always.

When a customer is frustrated, my first instinct is to understand what's actually happening. I want them to feel that I'm on their side, even when the solution isn't straightforward. Sometimes that means rethinking a subscription around a customer's budget rather than pushing for more.

At Chmura, I worked to retain at-risk accounts by exploring changes to subscription scope that better fit customer needs.
02 / COMMUNICATION

Be quick to respond.
Honest when it's complicated.

I know customers don't expect every answer instantly. They do want to know someone is paying attention. When an internal decision takes time, I keep checking in, sharing what I know, and following through instead of going quiet.

In work with the Virginia Department of Corrections and Goodwill-related workforce organizations, proactive updates helped maintain trust during delays.
03 / COLLABORATION

Bring good energy.
Lean on each other.

Strong customer relationships depend on strong internal ones. I like being someone who brings positive energy, does the research, asks thoughtful questions, and works with teammates who bring different strengths to the table.

When I spotted customer issues or workflow gaps, I brought findings and early recommendations to colleagues and leadership to build better next steps together.
What I believe
“By putting the client first, you're putting the company first.”

For me, that means earning trust before expecting loyalty. When customers feel understood, informed, and supported, stronger relationships and better business outcomes can follow.
