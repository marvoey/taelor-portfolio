How I protect and grow accounts.

Four examples, ordered by how closely they match the role: retention, outreach, growth, and ownership.

01 / CHURN SAVE

Turning a cancellation into a right-sized renewal

When customers faced cancellation over budget, I researched their budgets, service needs, and organizational structures before presenting practical alternatives. Options included removing lower-priority services, considering multi-year agreements, or identifying a parent organization's existing subscription they might join. The goal was to lower customer costs without losing the relationship.

~$20K in at-risk contract value retained

First 90 days

How I'd start at Nitra.

An outline of my approach, built around the role's goals of usage, retention, and expansion.

DAYS 1–30

Learn and listen

  • Get hands-on with the finance, marketplace, and AI products.
  • Review the book: segment accounts by usage, spend, and risk.
  • Listen to customer calls and shadow colleagues to learn what works.
DAYS 31–60

Act on the signals

  • Reach out to low- and zero-spend accounts to find what's blocking them.
  • Set up early-warning checks on daily usage data.
  • Start a first save playbook for at-risk accounts.
DAYS 61–90

Systemize and share

  • Turn what's working into repeatable outreach templates.
  • Track retention and consumption, and refine the playbooks.
  • Bring customer patterns back to product and sales.
